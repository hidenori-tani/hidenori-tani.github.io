Profile

Hidenori Tani,
Ph.D.

Associate Professor, School of Pharmacy, Yokohama University of Pharmacy. He trained as an engineer at Waseda University, took his doctorate a year early in 2008, and spent his postdoctoral years at the University of Tokyo, where he developed BRIC-seq — a method that measures the decay rate of every RNA in a human cell, genome-wide. He then spent eleven years at AIST, first as a Researcher and later as a Senior Researcher, working on human iPS cells, long noncoding RNAs and chemical safety assessment, and adding computational analysis to his bench work. He moved to Yokohama University of Pharmacy in 2023 to teach.

A longer first-person account of that path — why he chose research, and why he later turned toward teaching — is on the 日本語のページ.
